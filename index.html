01Membership

More than an advisory engagement

Cleveland Clinic Connected links hospitals and health organizations around the world with Cleveland Clinic's expertise. Membership brings four kinds of support together, so your teams can draw on Cleveland Clinic as needs change.

  • Operational advisory services

    Advice in four domains, scoped to your hospital. Our expertise

  • Education and training

    Observerships, fellowships, leadership development and simulation.

  • Provider-to-provider consultations

    Member physicians bring their most complex cases to Cleveland Clinic specialists through eClinical Review.

  • Co-branding and marketing support

    Use of the Cleveland Clinic Connected brand, and help developing marketing that shows your commitment to quality care.

What we've done with our partners

Highlights from Cleveland Clinic Connected members, drawn from public announcements by Cleveland Clinic and our partners.

Vietnam  |  Member since 2023

Vinmec Healthcare System

Since 2023, Cleveland Clinic leaders have worked on site at Vinmec Times City and Vinmec Central Park, reviewing quality, operations and patient safety. Cleveland Clinic sarcoma specialists led a bone cancer case workshop for about 100 clinicians and are supporting a new sarcoma center, and a 2025 epilepsy seminar with Cleveland Clinic drew about 200 clinicians. In October 2026, Vinmec's leaders worked at Cleveland Clinic on training and on readiness for Vinmec Can Gio, the new 500-bed hospital Cleveland Clinic advises on.

Tashkent, Uzbekistan  |  Provisional member since 2025

Central Asian University Hospital

A new 120-bed university hospital in Tashkent, designed on the Cleveland Clinic model of care. Cleveland Clinic leaders toured the campus and simulation center in March 2025, and after the agreement that August, university and Akfa Medline hospital leaders completed Cleveland Clinic's Global Physician Leadership Immersion Program. A Cleveland Clinic delegation returned in 2026 for working sessions and training on the care model and clinical launch, and in October 2026 Cleveland Clinic's CEO spent two days in Tashkent, meeting doctors and residents.

Croatia  |  Member since 2025

Radiochirurgia Zagreb

The first European member of Cleveland Clinic Connected. The partnership was first announced in December 2024, and the hospital joined in 2025 after a review of its safety, quality and patient experience standards that it describes as lasting nearly two years. Its teams now talk with Cleveland Clinic two to three times a week and can bring their hardest cases to joint consultations, and two of its clinical leaders were selected for the Global Physician Leadership Immersion Program.

Cleveland Clinic experts reviewed Barton Health's cardiology, oncology and neurology services. Frequently asked questions

Does Cleveland Clinic provide care at member hospitals?

No. All care at member hospitals is provided by local care teams. Member physicians can consult Cleveland Clinic specialists on complex cases through eClinical Review.

Does Cleveland Clinic own or manage member hospitals?

No. Members keep full ownership and management of their facilities. Membership is a way to collaborate, share knowledge and take part in education programs.

Can member physicians refer patients to Cleveland Clinic?

Yes. Cleveland Clinic locations in the United States and abroad accept referrals from members, but a referral cannot be guaranteed: patient safety and patient preference decide where care takes place.

What does Cleveland Clinic look for in a member?

Like-minded healthcare organizations, in the United States and internationally, that want to grow, improve their practice and deliver a better experience for their patients.
