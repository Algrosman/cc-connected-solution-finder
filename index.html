Membership

More than an advisory engagement

Cleveland Clinic Connected links hospitals and health organizations around the world with Cleveland Clinic's expertise. For members, two benefits stand out:

  1. Provider-to-provider consultations

    Member physicians can bring their most complex cases to Cleveland Clinic specialists through eClinical Review. See the eClinical Review offering

  2. Co-branding and marketing support

    Use of the Cleveland Clinic Connected brand, and help developing marketing that shows your commitment to quality care.

Frequently asked questions

Is advisory support available without membership?

In select cases, yes. Organizations that do not want a branded affiliation may be able to engage Cleveland Clinic for flexible, short-term advisory support, often as a first step toward a longer relationship. Contact us to check eligibility.

Does Cleveland Clinic provide care at member hospitals?

No. All care at member hospitals is provided by local care teams. Member physicians can consult Cleveland Clinic specialists on complex cases through eClinical Review.

Does Cleveland Clinic own or manage member hospitals?

No. Members keep full ownership and management of their facilities. Membership is a way to collaborate, share knowledge and take part in education programs.

Can member physicians refer patients to Cleveland Clinic?

Yes. Cleveland Clinic locations in the United States and abroad accept referrals from members, but a referral cannot be guaranteed: patient safety and patient preference decide where care takes place.

What does Cleveland Clinic look for in a member?

Like-minded healthcare organizations, in the United States and internationally, that want to grow, improve their practice and deliver a better experience for their patients.

Not sure where to start?

Our team works with hospital owners, ministries and health systems around the world. Tell us what you are planning and we will help you shape the right engagement.
