Membership

More than an advisory engagement

Cleveland Clinic Connected links hospitals and health organizations around the world with Cleveland Clinic's expertise. Membership brings four kinds of support together, so your teams can draw on Cleveland Clinic as needs change.

  1. Operational advisory services

    Finance, safety, quality and patient experience, and digital tools and analytics, scoped to your hospital.

    See our advisory expertise

  2. Education and training

    Observerships, fellowships, leadership development, simulation, faculty development and expert sessions.

    See our education offerings

  3. Provider-to-provider consultations

    Member physicians bring their most complex cases to Cleveland Clinic specialists through eClinical Review.

  4. Co-branding and marketing support

    Use of the Cleveland Clinic Connected brand, and help developing marketing that shows your commitment to quality care.

Our advisory expertise

Four domains, each drawing on how Cleveland Clinic runs its own hospitals, with solutions for new hospitals and for hospitals already caring for patients.

Finance

Financial strategy, planning and performance advice from an operating health system: how to advance your strategy, where to allocate capital for the greatest impact, and how to run a finance function that fits the organization.

For new hospitals

  • Feasibility, business cases and funding plans for new facilities and programs
  • Financial planning, budgeting and reporting in place before opening

For existing hospitals

  • Margin improvement, benchmarking and service line profitability
  • Partnerships, transactions and finance organization design

Safety, Quality, and Patient Experience (SQPE)

Cleveland Clinic's approach to safety, quality and patient experience, applied to your hospital through assessment, practical guidance and training.

For new hospitals

  • Governance, metrics and policies built in before opening and accreditation
  • Practice guides and training for a new workforce

For existing hospitals

  • A year-round program that benchmarks performance against Cleveland Clinic standards
  • Maturity assessments and train-the-trainer programs that build lasting capability

Digital Tools & Analytics

Vendor-neutral advice on the strategy, systems and governance behind a digital hospital, from a health system that runs them every day.

For new hospitals

  • A digital roadmap tied to the clinical plan and the opening date
  • Infrastructure, EHR and activation readiness for a safe go-live

For existing hospitals

  • EHR optimization, interoperability and cybersecurity
  • Data and analytics foundations, AI governance and the digital patient journey

Education

Cleveland Clinic education for physicians, nurses, pharmacists and leaders, at Cleveland Clinic, at Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi or at your hospital.

For new hospitals

  • Leadership onboarding and a culture program for the founding workforce
  • Simulation training and systems testing before opening

For existing hospitals

  • Observerships, fellowships and leadership development
  • Expert sessions, visiting faculty and specialist input on complex cases

Frequently asked questions

Does Cleveland Clinic provide care at member hospitals?

No. All care at member hospitals is provided by local care teams. Member physicians can consult Cleveland Clinic specialists on complex cases through eClinical Review.

Does Cleveland Clinic own or manage member hospitals?

No. Members keep full ownership and management of their facilities. Membership is a way to collaborate, share knowledge and take part in education programs.

Can member physicians refer patients to Cleveland Clinic?

Yes. Cleveland Clinic locations in the United States and abroad accept referrals from members, but a referral cannot be guaranteed: patient safety and patient preference decide where care takes place.

What does Cleveland Clinic look for in a member?

Like-minded healthcare organizations, in the United States and internationally, that want to grow, improve their practice and deliver a better experience for their patients.
